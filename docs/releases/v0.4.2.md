# Nest Climate Monitor v0.4.2

Personal-use prerelease for Android 10 or newer. Download **NestClimateMonitor-v0.4.2.apk**
and its `.apk.sha256` checksum below. This is an independent, read-only project, not an
official Google/Nest product.

## What's new

- Graph widgets fill their content box instead of collapsing into a skinny strip. The launcher
  selects a suitable portrait/landscape bitmap even when the app and home screen differ in orientation.
- The graph queries the complete six-hour window, without a total-row limit cutting off history.
  Lines crossing the left boundary retain their earlier saved sample, eliminating artificial gaps.
  Clipping only changes drawing; it does not create readings or extrapolate missing history.
- Indoor lines remain solid; outdoor lines and their legend samples are dashed, matching the app.
- A lone measurement appears as a dot. Sample-data picker previews contain no personal history
  or settings; actual preview appearance can vary with the launcher.

The widget retains its six-hour view; the app retains full-day history. Sampling schedules,
connections, saved readings and settings are unchanged.

## Installation and setup

Official releases use the same permanent signing key; this APK has versionCode 6.
**Developer/debug installations use a different key.** Never uninstall a working app merely
to test an official release. Export CSV before any intentional migration and reconnect Google
after switching signing identities.

Follow the [README](https://github.com/BenK22/NestClimateMonitor#readme). Each user supplies their
own Google project/OAuth credentials. Device Access registration costs a one-time **US$5**.
OAuth apps in Testing generally need Nest authorization renewed after about seven days in
**Settings → Nest Device Access → 3. Connect Nest**; saved client/project values remain valid.

Device Access supports screen-off/locked background requests. Google Home is attempted only
while the screen is on and the phone unlocked. Android may delay the requested 15-minute
interval; exact timing is not guaranteed. Outdoor readings are Open-Meteo model estimates.

## Validation and limitations

The widget corrections passed 66 full-SDK JVM tests, 11 isolated device tests and debug lint/build,
including a real Android widget-host orientation regression. The updated development APK was
checked on the maintainer's Pixel 9a/Nova home screen without losing readings or connection settings.
Official release packaging separately checks the permanent signer, version, non-debuggable state
and generated license resources.

See [the validation record](https://github.com/BenK22/NestClimateMonitor/blob/main/docs/VALIDATION.md).
Separate-account onboarding and a separate official APK-to-APK update test remain maintainer-waived,
not performed. Overnight sampling/recovery and actual Nova picker-preview inspection remain follow-ups.
Broader thermostat/launcher compatibility is not established. This remains a prerelease;
SDK-free CI alone does not validate Google's Home SDK or another account's setup.
